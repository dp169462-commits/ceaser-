⚙️ Algorithm Mechanics: Caesar Cipher

Encryption Formula

$$E_n(x) = (x + n) \pmod{26}$$ Where $x$ is the zero-based alphabet index of the letter ($A=0, B=1, \dots, Z=25$) and $n$ is the shift key.

Decryption Formula

$$D_n(x) = (x - n + 26) \pmod{26}$$ Guarantees non-negative results before modulo reduction.
